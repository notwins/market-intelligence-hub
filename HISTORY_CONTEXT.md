# History visibility and event examples

The default history view now combines the unchanged Federal Reserve/FRED monthly series with the nine SGS tenors already present in the original workbook. The source dropdown still offers each original feed separately. SGS controls display “SGS” and SGD explicitly; their source remains the supplied MAS-labelled workbook through August 2026, not a newly connected or revalidated MAS feed. No market values or observations were added or altered. Gaps are not filled. Live MAS refresh remains unconnected.

Twelve existing policy/trade events now have permanent sample charts below the main historical views. Each has a short original explanation and its official source link, rechecked on 5 November 2026. Two panels show UST/SGS 10-year yields and USD/SGD plus the derived CHF/SGD cross. Every window uses 12 calendar months before, the event month, and 12 months after (25 possible monthly observations), not exact day-to-day event returns. The fixed sample windows are independent of controls above. Incomplete coverage is labelled; monthly averages do not establish causation. The source tables retain original FX levels; chart FX values share a common rebasing month. Announcement/effective dates are distinguished for December 2013 taper, September 2017 runoff and July 2018 tariffs.

SPX was absent from both existing history datasets. A separate SPX tab now links to the verified FRED/S&P source and states that the in-page dataset is not connected. Its source notice requires prior written permission for reproduction; no restricted history, proxy or synthetic prices were published. The price index is distinguished from a yield and total return. Integrating data requires an appropriate licensed/public-display source.

History key labels, event markers and dates use accessible teal/purple emphasis. Switching history tabs preserves the tabs' viewport position rather than jumping to the top; browser back/forward remains usable and keyboard focus moves to the selected tab without scrolling.
