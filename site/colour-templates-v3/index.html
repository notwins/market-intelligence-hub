02 / Exact source colours

All 150 swatches

Five cores per family, each in Light, Original and Dark. These are custom Sanzo Wada-inspired expansions, not historical 15-colour sets from his book.

Shade is not display mode. Light / Original / Dark are shade variants, not light-mode/dark-mode text tokens. Many swatches are unsuitable for normal text on white. Use the prescribed theme foregrounds and test actual contrast.

03 / Theme selection

Follow the question first

83 subject routes and six precedence rules from the V3 master. Choose deliberately; do not infer a report’s meaning from its colours.

  1. Explicit choiceUse the requested article theme.
  2. Primary thesisWhat question is the report answering?
  3. InstrumentBond, share, option, FX or commodity.
  4. SectorWhen the instrument is not primary.
  5. GeographyWhen the location itself is the lens.
  6. Mixed researchNeutral Earth & Slate 03 fallback.

Example: a US AI company’s bond financing report follows the bond thesis, not an AI-equity background. An options report uses Deep Vintage 05; it does not inherit Singapore colours merely because its reader is in Singapore.

04 / Keyword meaning

Tag what the evidence supports

The exact V1.2 canonical tags, aliases, priorities and semantic HEX values remain authoritative.

Resolve context before colour

Use valid whole phrases and exact tickers with company context. Never match US inside USD or Fed inside FedEx. Do not select a security or share class from a company-name mention alone.

Keep ambiguity visible

BOC can mean Bank of Canada or Bank of China. Short codes such as C, SE, AI and DE require context. If identity remains unresolved, leave it unmapped rather than guess.

Respect precedence

Context first, then the longest valid phrase. Numeric priority resolves equal spans only after identity is valid. Preserve the reconciled source precedence: original V1, then specific source extensions, then generic fallback.

Separate the layers

A story may have several independent tags. A text span has one dominant semantic colour. Entity, geography, instrument, editorial status and market direction are separate concepts.

07 / Future publication

The publishing gate

Design rules belong beside evidence checks. Machine validation is a safety net, not a claim that an article’s facts or interpretation are correct.

Checked by the publication validator

  • New authored research HTML must carry the required article contract.
  • Theme and route must agree with the verified V3 standard, or carry an explicit permitted override declaration.
  • Declared presentation tokens and semantic tag colours must match their source.
  • Required visible title, takeaways, source links, cutoff, limitations and chart/card context must exist.
  • The final staged Pages artifact is checked again before deployment.

Still requires editorial review

  • Whether a route fits the actual thesis and whether an alias really identifies the entity.
  • Source reliability, factual accuracy, calculations, contradictory evidence and forecast honesty.
  • Rendered contrast, mobile readability, clipping, greyscale distinction and interactive behaviour.
  • A completed review declaration is not proof that human judgement was correct.

Existing reports are preserved. Their fixed legacy inventory is not retroactively restyled. New research pages need the contract; new non-article tools do not become research by using this template. Existing generated watchlist reports retain their separate archive, renderer and receipt checks until migrated; they are not certified as V3. Other file formats and external websites still need their applicable editorial review.
