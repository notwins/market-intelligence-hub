Financial scales have their own meaning

Yield level is not gain or loss. Treasury yield heat scales run from light to darker blue, with numbered bands and exact values. Negative yields are flagged separately in amber; unavailable values are grey. The range belongs to its loaded dataset and stays fixed when filtered.
