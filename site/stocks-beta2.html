    Data and coverage

    The 113 saved monthly editions run from July 2017 to November 2026. The archive has no editions from December 2016 to June 2017. Individual securities have coverage gaps. Share classes and exchange listings rank individually.

    Quote dates follow the archive’s price column. Prices retain the source quote currency and reflect the original share basis. GBp is UK pence. Market cap is in USD billions. The forward multiples and estimates use the coverage report’s definitions. Implied EPS is the price divided by positive forward P/E.
