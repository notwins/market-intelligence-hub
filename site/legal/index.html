The rules behind the research, models and decisions.

These pages explain what Market Intelligence Hub and its related tools are for, what their numbers can and cannot tell you, and how information is handled. The aim is useful research with visible assumptions, not fine print that hides the risks.

What this covers

This legal hub is drafted for the Market Intelligence Hub, its research articles and market reports, Portfolio Simulator V6 and V7, options and structured-product tools, and related bond, FX, CPF, retirement, insurance and market calculators operated or expressly adopted by the same project maintainer.

An independently operated third-party website linked from an article is not automatically covered. A product-specific notice takes precedence for the relevant product if it clearly states so.

The important distinction

  • Research is not personal advice. A public opinion about Treasury duration, Singapore banks or the gold market does not assess your individual circumstances.
  • Simulation is not a forecast. Portfolio outputs are mathematical consequences of assumptions, not future prices, suitability assessments or promises of return.
  • Data is not always live. Yield, bond price, FX, coupon, loan rate and corporate information can differ by source, session, date, settlement basis and instrument.
  • Not all tools handle data in the same way. Some Hub preferences and notes are saved in your browser. Storage and processing in separately hosted V6/V7 tools must be checked independently.

How readers should use the site

Use the articles to form better questions and the calculators to compare scenarios. Check the original issuer documents, official data, current transaction terms, your actual cashflows and the suitability of any product. Investment decisions should not depend solely on a single page or model.
