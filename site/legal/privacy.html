3. What has been checked in the Hub

A review of the public Market Intelligence Hub V4 source on 6 November 2026 identified use of browser localStorage for some settings, saved security selections, watchlists and workspace notes. The Hub also fetches public data files for research displays. Data held in browser storage can remain on that device until removed by the user, application or browser.

This finding does not prove that every tool is browser-only. The separately hosted Portfolio Simulator V6/V7, Options Lab, external data feeds, embedded content and any future features require their own technical checks. We cannot responsibly promise that all portfolio inputs “never leave your device” without that verification.

4. How information is used

Where applicable, information is used to deliver the pages and features you request, remember your on-device preferences, calculate entered scenarios, respond to correspondence, investigate errors, protect the service, and meet applicable legal obligations. Personal data should not be collected for unrelated marketing or sold by the project operator without a separate, properly disclosed basis.

If analytics, newsletters, feedback forms, login accounts, advertising, cloud synchronisation or AI APIs are added, this policy must be updated before deployment to describe their purposes and relevant recipients; any consent or controls required by applicable law must also be implemented.

5. Browser storage, cookies and analytics

Browser localStorage is different from an HTTP cookie. Clearing site data for the relevant domain may remove saved preferences and notes. Blocking local storage may stop parts of a workspace from working or prevent persistence.

Hosting services and third-party websites may use cookies, request logs or similar technologies. No statement is made here that analytics or tracking are absent from the entire suite, because every separately hosted tool has not been audited. If non-essential analytics or advertising trackers are deployed, provide an accurate disclosure and appropriate choice mechanisms for the relevant jurisdictions.

6. Hosting, transfers and outside websites

The public Hub is hosted using GitHub Pages; certain linked tools are served from different hosting domains. When you access an online service, its hosting provider can process network and technical information required to deliver the site. Some processing may occur outside Singapore. External market-data sites, news publishers, chart services or AI services may process data under their own terms when opened or used.

Any transfer of personal data controlled by the project operator must satisfy applicable requirements, including Singapore's PDPA where it applies. We do not guarantee that external services operate under Singapore law.

7. Disclosure, retention and safeguards

Information may be available to infrastructure and technical providers insofar as needed to run the relevant service, or disclosed where lawfully required. The final notice should list material providers and categories of data once the technical audit is complete.

Browser-stored information remains subject to browser and app behaviour; clearing it can make stored workspaces unrecoverable. Operator-held correspondence should be kept only while reasonably needed for the stated purpose or legal requirements. Hosting logs may follow the provider's retention schedule. We use or will implement reasonable safeguards appropriate to information actually handled, but no internet service can guarantee perfect security.

8. Your choices and rights

You can generally delete the Hub's device-specific preferences by clearing saved data for the site in your browser. Before doing so, export or copy anything you wish to retain. For personal data held by the operator, you may request information, access, correction or withdrawal of consent where applicable under Singapore law, using the private contact stated above. Some requests may be limited by legal exceptions or information the operator does not possess.

Do not publish identifiable portfolio information or privacy requests in public comments or GitHub issues. For information kept only in your browser, the operator may not have a copy to provide or delete remotely.

9. Children and sensitive financial information

These financial research and modelling tools are not designed to solicit personal information from children. The operator should not deliberately seek highly sensitive identifiers or confidential client data through general-access tools. If such information is accidentally received, it should be handled according to applicable law, institutional obligations and a documented incident procedure.
