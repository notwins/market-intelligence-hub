This website is a research and financial-literacy project. Read the following before relying on an article, market dashboard, calculator or portfolio simulation.

1. General circulation, not personal advice

Information and opinions on the Market Intelligence Hub and related publications are intended for general circulation and educational use. They do not take into account the specific investment objectives, financial situation, needs, experience, risk tolerance or tax position of any particular person.

Nothing here should be treated, merely because it appears on the site, as personalised financial advice, investment management, a suitability assessment, or a solicitation or offer to buy or sell any security, fund, bond, option, structured product, insurance policy, foreign currency or other financial product. Obtain advice from an appropriately qualified and authorised financial adviser about suitability for your circumstances before committing to an investment product.

Public commentary may discuss particular securities, entry prices, possible trades, hedges or portfolio allocations. A disclaimer alone does not decide whether particular content amounts to regulated financial advice under Singapore law. The substance, context, audience and manner of distribution matter.

2. Independent project; no institutional endorsement

Unless expressly identified otherwise, publications should be understood as the opinions or analysis of the independent project maintainer, not official communications from any employer, financial institution, regulator, issuer or data provider. A reference to OCBC, MAS, SNB, the Federal Reserve or another institution does not imply its approval or sponsorship.

Professional titles or experience mentioned by a writer do not transform these web publications into authorised institutional research, nor should this site be mistaken for an official bank channel. Information obtained under client confidentiality, employment restrictions or third-party licensing must not be published without proper authorisation.

3. Not a real-time quotation or execution service

Headlines, prices, yield curves, spreads, interest rates, foreign-exchange quotations and valuations may be delayed, indicative, estimated, rounded, revised, sourced from different dates, or affected by market hours and settlement conventions. A published yield is not necessarily a tradable executable price or the return an investor will receive after fees, taxes and FX movements.

Check every security identifier, quote convention, ex-coupon and settlement date, accrued interest, clean versus dirty price, minimum denomination, embedded option, liquidity, counterparty and transaction cost with the actual dealing platform or offering documentation before acting.

The website does not place orders, hold client assets, verify account balances or guarantee access to any market.

4. Models, calculators and Portfolio V6/V7

Portfolio simulations, bond calculators, retirement and CPF examples, insurance illustrations, options tools and structured-investment scenario analyses are hypothetical decision-support tools. Results depend on input quality, formulas, timing, accounting conventions and assumptions. Past performance, backtests, distributions and scenario paths do not establish future outcomes.

A scenario may exclude or simplify transaction costs, taxes, spreads, funding resets, early redemption, default, issuer call rights, liquidity, FX conversion, drawdowns, fees, policy charges and changing regulations. A return shown as an annualised rate may be materially different from realised cash profit or a money-weighted return. Different versions, including V6 and V7, may not use identical methodologies.

For leveraged simulations: portfolio net asset value, liabilities, financing cashflows, FX translation and capital outlay must be kept separate. Do not assume an illustration captures margin calls, financing constraints or all contractual obligations.

5. AI-assisted research and automated updates

Some content, categorisation, charts, summaries, translations or code may be AI-assisted or automatically produced. Such outputs can contain false statements, incorrect calculations, incomplete sources, stale data, misunderstood context and plausible-looking but invalid citations. A “verified” label should be used only for figures and statements actually checked against their cited sources.

Research updates and corrections may be published later. The site is not obliged to maintain a historical version or notify readers of every change. Check the article's publication date, any correction notice, data cutoff and underlying source.

6. Market and product risks

All investments carry risks, potentially including complete loss of invested capital. Leveraged positions can generate losses beyond the initial cash outlay or require additional funding. Foreign-currency investments involve translation and funding risk even where the underlying instrument pays its coupon or dividend as expected.

Products discussed may be complex, unsuitable for some investors, or unavailable in certain jurisdictions. Read the dedicated Investment & Modelling Risks page and the actual prospectus, term sheet, product highlights sheet or insurance policy terms.
