How price moves are calculated

Returns are rolling price changes in the listing currency and exclude dividends. Day columns count trading sessions. Week, month and year columns look back from the latest saved history close. Older lookbacks can use saved weekly or monthly period values. Each return cell gives its source dates. A dash means history is unavailable. Latest price shows the latest available quote and its own date.
