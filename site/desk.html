How headlines are selected

Each review keeps up to five stories. Credit stress, including delinquencies, defaults and widening spreads, ranks first. Fed, SNB and MAS policy, Treasury auctions and yields, and material moves in bonds and credit also receive priority.

Watchlist companies and major issuers qualify for developments such as earnings, financing, acquisitions or regulatory decisions. A smaller borrower can qualify when its story signals wider credit stress. Stories matching several priorities rank higher. Earlier selections stay searchable.

How price moves are calculated

Returns are rolling price changes in the listing currency and exclude dividends. Day columns count trading sessions. Older lookbacks can use saved weekly or monthly values. Yearly uses imported daily closes when a matching listing is available and shows the imported quote date for that row. Each return cell gives its source dates. Empty cells and dashes indicate unavailable history.
