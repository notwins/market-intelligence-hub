New results: Data Center drives record Q3; Q4 revenue guide rises

Reported facts

  • Data Center revenue was $8.546 billion, up 97%; Client and Gaming revenue fell 8% to $3.722 billion. Q4 guidance is $17 billion revenue, plus or minus 3%, and approximately 55% non-GAAP gross margin versus 56% in Q3.
Research analysis
