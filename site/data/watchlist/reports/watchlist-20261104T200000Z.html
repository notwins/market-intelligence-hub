Google provides credit support for TeraWulf’s Anthropic campus

Reported facts

  • Earlier 04 Nov context newly collated in this edition; the original publication time below precedes the main 16:00–20:00 UTC update window.
  • TeraWulf’s original news listing timestamps its announcement at 08:15 EST on 04 Nov (13:15 UTC). It announced Google credit-support agreements connected to financing its Hawesville, Kentucky campus and approximately US$19 billion of contracted Anthropic leases. The US$19 billion is lease value, not a disclosed Google guarantee amount.
  • TeraWulf’s 8-K describes approximately 401 MW under the existing leases, with delivery expected in phases from late 2027 through Q2 2028. Google agreed to backstop certain Anthropic obligations, becoming effective at final-data-hall commencement under each corresponding lease, subject to contract conditions.
Research analysis

The arrangement extends Alphabet’s involvement in financing AI infrastructure beyond its own facilities. It is not evidence of immediate cash spending or new Alphabet revenue.

Risks and countercase

Contingent obligations, counterparty exposure and construction timing matter; the full lease value must not be treated as Google’s guaranteed exposure.

Next to watch

Financing completion, further contract disclosures and campus delivery milestones.
