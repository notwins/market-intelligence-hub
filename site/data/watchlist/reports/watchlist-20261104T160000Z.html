Amazon opens a free Shop Direct portal for outside merchants

Reported facts

  • Amazon’s original release, timestamped 14:00:07 UTC, introduces Shop Direct Merchant Workspace, a free self-service portal through which eligible merchants can connect product feeds to Amazon and Alexa for Shopping.
  • Amazon says tens of millions of customers have discovered hundreds of millions of outside-store products through Shop Direct. Customers can be directed to the merchant’s website, while eligible products support Buy for Me. These reach figures are issuer claims.
Research analysis

The portal may strengthen Amazon as the starting point for shopping even when inventory sits elsewhere. Participation and discovery do not by themselves establish incremental revenue.

Risks and countercase

Merchant uptake, data accuracy, purchase conversion and customer experience determine commercial value. No financial contribution was quantified.

Next to watch

Merchant adoption, conversion metrics and any disclosed monetisation or revenue contribution.

Reported facts
