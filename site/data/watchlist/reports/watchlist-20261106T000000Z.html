IREN discloses Microsoft-requested network changes and revised deployment timing

Reported facts

  • IREN says Microsoft requested a network design change across its Horizon deployments. Its 10-Q says delivery dates extend without delay credits for delays caused by that requested change; discussions on delivery dates, fees, credits and reimbursements continue.
  • IREN moved the target for more than $4 billion of operational annualized run-rate revenue from year-end 2026 to January 2027, possibly end-February, citing a customer-requested change and supplier timing. Horizon 1 was accepted by Microsoft in August and remains in service.
Research analysis

The new primary disclosure identifies delivery timing and contract mechanics relevant to Microsoft's external AI capacity, rather than establishing cancellation of an existing contract.

Risks and countercase

IREN's ARR is an operating metric, not GAAP revenue, and requires commissioning, testing and customer acceptance. The delay-credit relief described is specific to Microsoft-requested changes, not a blanket exemption for every delay.

Next to watch

Revised delivery schedule, customer acceptance and final treatment of fees, credits and cost reimbursements.
