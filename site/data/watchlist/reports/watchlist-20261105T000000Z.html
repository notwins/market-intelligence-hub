Reported facts

New prospectus confirms Alphabet’s €6 billion offering terms

Reported facts

  • Alphabet filed a prospectus supplement for €6 billion across seven senior unsecured tranches maturing in 2028–2045.
  • The prospectus is dated 2 November; expected delivery is on or about 5 November. This is a newly filed document for an earlier-priced deal, not evidence settlement has occurred.
  • Six fixed-rate coupons range from 3.70% to 5.25%; the €750 million floating-rate tranche pays three-month EURIBOR plus 0.50%.
Research analysis

Term details make financing cost and maturity structure clearer, but do not directly demonstrate AI project returns.

Risks and countercase

Currency, floating-rate interest and execution risks; avoid treating coupon as yield or expected delivery as completed settlement.

Next to watch

Confirmation of expected 5 November delivery and later financing/cashflow disclosures.
