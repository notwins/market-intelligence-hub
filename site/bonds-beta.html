Price, yield and return definitions

Bid prices are quoted per 100 face value in the bond currency. BOS and OCBC history uses the printed report date and reported yield to maturity. WSJ uses asked yields. Bondsupermart uses bid yields to maturity or to worst as labelled. A point represents the source observation only.

Yearly price change uses the first and last available bid in each calendar year within your selected range. It excludes coupons, accrued interest, fees and FX. ACE Fixed AR is the workbook snapshot percentage and has no historical time series.
