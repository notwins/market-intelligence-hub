RA formula and exclusions

Next balance = balance × (1 + base rate) + extra interest
RA-only extra = 2% × min(balance, 30,000) + 1% × min(max(balance − 30,000, 0), 30,000)

The formula is recalculated each complete year. Extra interest is capped at S$900/year here. Actual extra interest uses combined balances, prioritising RA (including CPF LIFE premium), with OA eligibility capped at S$20,000. This pre-payout model excludes OA/MA balances and extra OA interest that could flow into RA.

Read the numbers together

Each row is at the birthday, before the next payment. “Real” is monthly payout purchasing power. “Paid” is cumulative nominal payouts. “Remaining” is simplified unspent premium.

Income formulas and valuation timing

Level monthly amount at year n = starting level payout
Escalating monthly amount at year n = starting escalating payout × 1.02ⁿ
Real monthly amount = nominal amount ÷ (1 + inflation)ⁿ

Year 0 is the payout-start birthday, before any payment. Each completed year has 12 equal monthly payments; escalation happens on each anniversary. At age 85 with payouts from 65, the cumulative total includes exactly 240 payments, and the displayed monthly amount is the next year's rate. Real amounts are measured in payout-start purchasing power, not today's prices.

CPF LIFE payout amounts depend on member-specific actuarial factors and can be adjusted for long-term changes in interest rates or life expectancy. This arithmetic holds the supplied level amount fixed and the supplied escalating amount on a 2% path; it cannot replicate CPF's actuarial engine. It does not calculate an IRR or a “best” death age.

03 / THE MONEY FLOW

Why 4% interest is not a 4% payout yield

1. Retirement Account

Savings and eligible top-ups earn RA interest before CPF LIFE starts.

2. LIFE premium

For Standard and Escalating, RA savings are used as the annuity premium when payouts start.

3. Monthly income

Payouts include use of premium and support from the lifelong risk pool. Income continues for life.

4. On death

Any remaining premium plus remaining CPF account savings is distributed to beneficiaries.

The pooled interest supports longevity

Interest earned on CPF LIFE premiums is pooled to support lifelong payouts. It does not appear as a separately compounding pot that can simply be added to your individual bequest.

CPF LIFE is backed by the Singapore Government. This independent page and its figures are not official CPF projections.

Basic Plan is deliberately not simulated

Basic uses only part of RA savings as premium, with different account drawdown and payout mechanics. Applying the same simple remaining-premium curve would mislead.

See the official Basic Plan explanation. This page makes no numerical comparison with private insurance products.
