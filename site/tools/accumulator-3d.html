Reading the axes. Both strike and knock-out are percentages of the initial NVDA price. A 120% knock-out is 20% above that price. The guarantee is measured in months.

Reading the lines. Follow a solid line to change the guarantee at a fixed knock-out. Follow a dashed line to change the knock-out at a fixed guarantee. Only the dots carry a quoted price.

Reading the price. A lower strike reduces what you pay per delivered share. Your overall gain or loss also depends on how many shares you receive and what they are worth when you sell.
