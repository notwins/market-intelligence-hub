Illustrative maturity-only structure with a fixed simple coupon and no early redemption. Below strike, principal redemption equals principal × final gold price ÷ strike price. At or above strike, principal is repaid in full. Coupon income assumes the issuer pays throughout the tenor. Fees and issuer default are excluded.
