Calculation assumptions

Interest accrues at the annual rate divided by payments per year. Early repayments are applied after the scheduled principal and interest, capped at the outstanding balance. At a rate change, the instalment is reset. In Shorten tenure mode, it follows the instalment calculated without extra payments for that rate period. In Keep tenure mode, it uses the remaining balance and original maturity.

Results estimate principal and interest. Taxes, insurance, fees, penalties, and daily interest adjustments are excluded. Displayed money is rounded to whole units. The schedule uses full precision.
