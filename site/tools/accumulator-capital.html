Example inputs and trade terms

The starting examples use the Hub’s USD 165.05 NVDA close on 23 November 2026 and its 24 November 2026 indications for nine months, a 120% knock-out and one guaranteed month. The SI strike level is 95.89% and the SN level is 90.56%. The example monthly target is 100 shares. Replace the inputs with your quote.

A daily contract can settle monthly while accruing a different number of shares each month. A knock-out may shorten purchases, subject to any guaranteed period. If the market price falls below strike, you can still have to buy at the higher strike.
