    Model assumptions and sources

    Each synthetic year has 252 fixings. Historical replays use the available GLD closes. A close at or above the accumulator KO ends purchases and delivers any guaranteed shortfall. Below KO, purchases continue at the fixed strike even when GLD trades lower. The initial funding rounds up to a whole dollar.

    A decumulator sells daily at its fixed strike until maturity or a close at or below its lower KO. The optional 2× delivery applies only above the sale strike. Unsold shares remain exposed to GLD. Rollover and sale terms are hypothetical until the bank supplies fresh terms.

    Cash earns zero. The model assumes full funding, covered delivery, zero transaction costs and no tax, funding charge, counterparty default or early unwind. Accrued shares count as inventory. Confirm settlement, guarantee dates, KO+1, offsets, available collateral and the actual re-entry date with the bank before using delivered shares or a refund.

    GLD trades in USD per share. Spot gold (XAU/USD) is quoted per troy ounce. All contract observations here use GLD. Historical closes and dated term sheets come from the Hub.
