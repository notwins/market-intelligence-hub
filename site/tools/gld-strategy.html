How to read the plays and settlement assumptions

Price and time. Each path connects editable prices with daily closes across 252 sessions per model year. Every quote sees exactly the same GLD fixings. The XAU view applies the same percentage change to the starting spot gold price per troy ounce. GLD triggers always use USD per share.

Quote mechanics. A funded 1× accumulator buys the daily share lot below its upper KO, including below strike. At or above KO, it delivers any outstanding guaranteed shares and releases unused funding. Sizing rounds down to a fully fundable whole daily lot. A covered decumulator sells at its fixed strike until maturity or a lower KO. The starting sale example has no guaranteed deliveries.

Waiting and adding. From entry, waiting preserves the opening cash and shares. Staged buying invests the selected fraction of available cash immediately, then invests the remainder at the close after GLD first meets the dip threshold measured from entry. After KO the threshold resets to the KO close. Market buys, new rolls and covered sales observe the re-entry delay. A post-KO dip order also fills at the next close after its signal.

Decision timing. The leader has the highest eligible marked P&L on this particular path up to the inspected fixing. At KO, a second comparison measures additional P&L from the same cash and shares. It can take several sessions before the alternatives produce different outcomes.

Cash earns zero. The simulation excludes fees, tax, borrowing, counterparty failure and early unwind. Market moves between cash settlement and delivery can change the result.
