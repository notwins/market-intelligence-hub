SOURCE CONTEXT

Dates travel with the data

The archive holds original levels and native frequencies. Missing values remain gaps. Charts label growth, basis-point or index transformations.

ICE histories begin in November 2023. Other series have shorter coverage or publication lags. Monthly updates merge recent source revisions into the retained history. These are not point-in-time vintages.
