What the files preserve

Native source values are retained in full-history CSVs. Monthly updates merge a three-month overlap; earlier snapshots remain available. JSON exposes the same columns as raw strings, plus numeric chart points where available. The catalogue records SHA-256 hashes. The ZIP includes all 74 CSVs, both inventories and coverage notes.

Coverage and definitions

ICE data has about three years of available history. New York Fed figures are balances 90+ days delinquent. Treasury auctions include regular-term 10Y notes and normal reopenings. TIC includes country and aggregate rows; January 2007–2011 gaps remain missing.

Reusable sources

Each citation identifies the source, series, period and saved date. Dated file paths remain fixed for reproducible references. Source conditions and third-party attribution continue to apply.

Trepp office/multifamily and CME FedWatch histories were unavailable and are listed in the external inventory.
