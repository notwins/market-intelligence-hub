How rules and tags work

Parentheses run first, then NOT, then AND, then OR. Write "interest rates" for an exact phrase, tag:Google for a company tag, or text:earnings for saved words only. Use quotes around a multi-word tag: tag:"Government bonds".

Tags come from saved metadata and explicit company-name or ticker mentions in saved text. They are navigation aids, not an inferred investment exposure. The tag tree also contains proposed vocabulary that may not have any tagged content yet.

All matching saved records are available through Previous / Next. Search cannot see private chats, unuploaded reports, linked PDF contents, or the full bodies of external news articles.
