4. Consumer / Industrials

Union Pacific (UNP) | Industrial pricing power

Reuters’ 12 November analysis found that third-quarter fuel surcharges exceeded diesel costs by $131.8m, contributing $0.15 per share to earnings.

Through September, the surcharge surplus reached $188.1m, while rival BNSF’s surcharge collections were $922.4m below fuel costs.

This is a newly reported analysis of prior-quarter earnings, not a new Thursday earnings release. Higher energy prices can support a company’s profits when its contractual pricing recovers more than its actual costs. Sources: [11.1]

9. Three client talking points

01

Strong AI demand does not mean every AI company is a good investment.

Cisco, Applied Materials and Cerebras posted encouraging numbers, but Nebius fell 16.8% after warning of capacity delays.

Action: Distinguish infrastructure suppliers from debt-funded cloud operators. Check capital requirements, order delivery and cash conversion.

02

Improved efficiency can conceal emerging credit risk.

Nubank improved its cost outlook while its 90-day delinquency ratio rose to 7.3%.

Action: Assess loan growth, credit losses, provisions and risk-adjusted profitability separately from revenue or headline net income.

03

Inflation remains a major test for both equities and bonds.

Oil rose sharply on Thursday and Friday’s producer-price report remains ahead.

Action: Reassess duration and leveraged exposure using actual yields and FX levels before making portfolio changes.
