XAU / GOLD · Q3 2026 IN BRIEF

Central banks bought more.
Gold prices still fell.

Central-bank demand can support gold, while your return still depends on the market price, currency movements and costs.

Three things to know

Official buying increased. The World Gold Council estimated 321.2 tonnes of net buying in Q3 2026. Net buying means purchases less sales by central banks and other official institutions. [1]

Some buyers remain unidentified. Public disclosures accounted for 120 tonnes within that total estimate. The estimates can be revised as more information arrives. [1]

Your spending currency matters. For a holding without currency protection, gold’s USD price and the USD-to-SGD exchange rate both affect your SGD return. A stronger SGD can reduce or even erase a USD gold gain.

Risks and your next step

Gold can fall alongside other investments. Higher bond returns after inflation and a stronger US dollar can pressure gold prices. Physical gold pays no coupon, and dealing, fund or storage costs reduce returns. [3]

Decide the purpose before buying. Consider how long you can hold gold, cash you may need in SGD, and the product’s costs and how it handles currency risk.
