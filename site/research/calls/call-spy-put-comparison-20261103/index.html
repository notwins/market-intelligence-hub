What the comparison means

Strategy A was an ownership-oriented illustration; Strategy B was a leveraged premium-harvesting illustration. The first structure requires willingness and funding to buy 100 shares at the strike. The ten-put structure can create a US$600,000 purchase obligation even when the initial buying-power requirement is much lower.

The reported tenor for Strategy B was 58 days, not roughly 40 days. Preserve the 31 December 2026 expiry and the ticket's displayed day count. Do not compare the two premiums as though they were earned over the same period.

Risks and limits

Buying-power effect is not maximum loss and is not guaranteed to remain fixed. A fall in SPY or a rise in implied volatility can increase short-option losses and margin demands before expiry. Early assignment and forced liquidation are possible.

All returns are conditional and before costs. The original quote reference was SPY US$751.96. The assumed limit premiums do not prove that either trade filled. The report makes no claim about current availability, current pricing or personal account balances.
