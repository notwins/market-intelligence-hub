Purpose and outcome

The plan was temporary protection through an earnings window, with an intended exit one week after entry. A longer option expiry was chosen to reduce estimated short-holding-period time decay. The stated purpose was protection against a repricing associated with AI capital spending, cash-flow pressure and debt-service concerns, rather than a claim that a particular company would fail.

The later update showed a larger dollar gain but a smaller proportion of the illustrative stock loss offset. The report's QQQ-equivalent portfolio comparison showed 47.68% of the reference loss offset, versus 75.39% in the earlier review. Actual stock holdings and their returns were not established.

Costs and limitations

Time decay was one component of cost, not a fixed insurance bill. Starting Greeks suggested approximately US$660 of seven-day theta for the eight-put example if price and implied volatility were unchanged. Price moves, volatility changes, bid-ask spreads and execution costs could produce very different results.

These snapshots do not establish a completed one-week hedge or a realized return. The latest midpoint would imply a US$14,908 gain, but midpoint is not an assured fill. Fees, financing, dividends, tax and slippage are excluded, and a QQQ proxy cannot establish the performance of a different stock portfolio.
