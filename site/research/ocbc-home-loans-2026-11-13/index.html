Fixed or SORA-linked?

Fixed (completed properties only): the rate stays unchanged during the fixed period. Confirm what applies afterwards.

3M Compounded SORA: the benchmark is reset on the package’s review dates. Your payment can change. Ask for the applicable spread and reset schedule.

Package categories: OCBC’s purchase and refinancing pages linked alongside.

Compare the whole package

A lower opening rate is only part of the decision. Compare lock-in, partial repayment, early redemption, conversion charges, legal or valuation costs and any subsidy clawback against how long you expect to keep the loan.

Fixed pricing offers budgeting certainty for its fixed period. SORA-linked pricing can move either way; neither is always cheaper.

Ask for a dated quotation

Request every yearly rate, the later SORA spread, minimum loan, fixed and lock-in periods, fees, prepayment terms, rewards and clawbacks. Confirm quotation expiry and eligibility.

For repricing, use Loans → loan account → Loan repricing in the OCBC app. Check eligibility with the bank and review the Supplemental Letter of Offer and Fact Sheet before accepting.
