AI’s financing loop

When suppliers also finance their customers, a single setback can hit sales, investments and guarantees at the same time. That warrants closer underwriting. It does not prove a systemic crisis.

Three points to know

  • 55.2% is a share of disclosed deal value, not cash contributed by AI firms. The BIS measure counts entire funding rounds that involved AI investors, not each investor’s contribution.
  • Notional amounts and maximum guarantees are not losses. Alphabet, Broadcom and NVIDIA disclose different forms of support, with different triggers, timing and recoveries.
  • Watch the cash coming from customers. Financing can help build capacity, but it cannot by itself show that demand is profitable or self-sustaining.

Limits: The BIS sample covers 2021–2025. The issuer disclosures are dated and cannot be added together as one exposure total. A jump in CDS trading volume is not the same as a jump in default probability. Educational research, not personalised advice.
