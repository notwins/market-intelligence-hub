Income is not the same as return

PIMCO Income · UST 2041 · Temasek 2070

  • A bond fund is not a bond held to maturity.
  • A distribution, a coupon and a yield to maturity measure different things.
  • Hedging currency does not remove interest-rate or credit risk.

Compare the structure first

PIMCO Income is a managed portfolio. It does not promise repayment of your purchase price at one maturity date. Distributions are not guaranteed; NAV changes and costs also affect your return. Confirm the exact share class, currency, hedging policy and charges.

A 2041 US Treasury is a direct bond exposure, once identified. The public report does not verify the precise CUSIP, coupon or maturity date. It therefore supplies no instrument-specific current price, yield or duration.

Temasek Series 21 is long-dated corporate debt, not Singapore Government debt. Its contractual coupon is 2.50%, with maturity on 6 October 2070. Temasek explicitly states its debt is not guaranteed by the Singapore Government.

One simple distinction

A 2.50% coupon means US$2.50 annual coupon cash for US$100 face value. It is not a current yield quote. The price you pay, timing of cash flows, costs, currency and issuer performance affect your outcome.

What still changes the outcome?

Lower government yields can help fixed-rate bond prices, but wider credit spreads can offset that benefit. A currency hedge addresses exchange-rate exposure, not bond-price losses. Subtracting one assumed hedge charge from a headline yield does not establish a realised SGD return.
