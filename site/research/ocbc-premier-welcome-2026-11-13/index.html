OCBC Premier welcome rewards

Check the conditions before counting a welcome reward as a benefit.

Source check: 2026-11-13T07:04:31+00:00

S$800 card credit with at least S$350,000 qualifying fresh funds. An additional S$1,000 requires individual Accredited Investor qualification, opt-in within three months from the Onboarding Month, and AI status at fulfilment.

Who qualifies?

Join during 6–30 November 2026 and sign the programme terms. The month the relationship starts is your Onboarding Month. Existing Premier/Private Client relationships, including International Wealth, and those closed within the previous 12 months are excluded.

Funding and holding

Place funds as primary holder from the month before through the month after onboarding. Maintain the qualifying amount for three months after the latest placement month.

Fresh funds must be incremental and pass the 30-day non-recycling test. Promotional funds are excluded except OCBC Time Deposits.

How rewards arrive

Credit goes to Premier VOYAGE or Premier Visa Infinite within eight weeks after the later maintenance-period/onboarding-month endpoint. Card approval and annual fees apply; supplementary-card rewards go to the principal holder.

Before committing

One reward of each type per eligible client. Ineligibility can trigger clawback. Other promotions cannot generally be combined; confirm the Time Deposit exception and exact dates with OCBC.
