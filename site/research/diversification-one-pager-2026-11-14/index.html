DIVERSIFICATION / ONE-PAGE BRIEF

Don't rely on one investment.

Spread your money across investments with different drivers, so one disappointment has less power over your plans.

  • Spread the risk. Diversify across businesses, markets and investment types. [1,2]
  • Check what you really own. Different funds may hold the same companies. [3]
  • Give each part a job. Balance long-term growth, income and accessible money for upcoming needs. [1,2]

Keep the limits in view

Diversification can still lose money. Investments can fall together and the mix may trail a concentrated winner. A risky addition can raise overall risk. Currency movements, costs and access restrictions matter. [1-3]

Your next step

Review your portfolio before adding another product. Check repeated exposures, keep upcoming spending accessible and agree a suitable mix and review rule. [1-3]
