9. Three client talking points

01

AI demand is real, but the market is now testing funding and execution.

Cisco, Applied Materials and Cerebras exceeded expectations while Nebius reduced guidance.

Action: Favour analysis of actual cash receipts, project completion, gross margins and debt obligations over contract headlines alone.

02

An efficient bank is not automatically a safer lender.

Nubank improved its efficiency outlook but reported a higher delinquency ratio.

Action: Assess credit losses and loan-book quality together with growth, costs and profitability.

03

Next week's Nvidia results may be a more important market catalyst than Friday's earnings beats.

Leadership, financing relationships and spending expectations are now directly influencing AI equities.

Action: Avoid treating an isolated share-price rebound as confirmation of a durable recovery. Maintain separate risk limits for individual shares, bonds and leveraged positions.
