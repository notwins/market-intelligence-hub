Matching periods

Market series use the last available close in a calendar month. FRED monthly series and the Pink Sheet retain the source’s monthly observation. A partial latest month can appear. Annual comparisons match by calendar year and use December gold when available. Observations use their economic period and can be published later.

Returns and rates

Price, FX and index returns are percent changes. Yields and inflation rates are compared as percentage-point changes. The matrix uses paired changes across consecutive reported months with at least 12 pairs. Correlation describes co-movement over the selected window.

Stocks, flows and local currency

Spot XAU/USD is gold per troy ounce. GLD trades per share, and Nasdaq’s XAU is a mining equity index. IMF official holdings measure a stock in tonnes. Mine output measures tonnes produced during a year. FX translation joins the daily gold and FX records on the exact date. U.S. real gold divides the Pink Sheet price by CPI and expresses it in dollars of the latest available CPI month.
