    Trace the data

    Coverage dates and source links follow the individual series. TIPS begin in 2003. The Hub’s S&P 500 close history starts in 2006. Official holdings and China series follow their own reporting windows.

    Matching periods

    Market series use the last available close in a calendar month. FRED monthly series and the Pink Sheet retain the source’s monthly observation. A partial latest month can appear. Annual comparisons use December gold when available. For quarterly comparisons, the World Bank and real-gold views use the mean of the three monthly gold observations. Close-based series use the final month’s close. Observations use their economic period and can be published later.

    Returns and rates

    Price, FX and index returns are percent changes. Yields and inflation rates use percentage-point changes. Signed flows and spreads expressed in other units use differences in those units. The matrix uses paired changes across consecutive reported months with at least 12 pairs. Correlation describes co-movement over the selected window.

    Stocks, flows and local currency

    Spot XAU/USD is gold per troy ounce. GLD trades per share, and Nasdaq’s XAU is a mining equity index. IMF official holdings measure a stock in tonnes. Mine output measures tonnes produced during a year. FX translation joins the daily gold and FX records on the exact date. U.S. real gold divides the Pink Sheet price by CPI and expresses it in dollars of the latest available CPI month.

    Source releases vary by series and may revise past observations. The catalogue loads public extracts directly, with exact source links alongside the saved series. World Bank data are attributed to the World Bank.
